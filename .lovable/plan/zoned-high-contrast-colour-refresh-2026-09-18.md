# Zoned high-contrast colour refresh

## Direction
Use the selected **Zoned high-contrast rhythm** across the site. Purple, yellow, and orange become the only expressive colours; clean white and near-black remain as neutral breathing space and for accessible text.

The restraint comes from one rule: **one dominant poppy colour per section**. The other two colours appear only as small accents, rules, labels, or actions.

## Homepage colour rhythm

1. **Opening: poppy purple**
   - Keep the full purple opening and white headline.
   - Keep yellow for the highlighted words and primary action.
   - Use orange only as a very small detail, not a competing block.

2. **Selected work: near-black**
   - Replace the beige background with near-black and switch all copy to white or soft neutral white.
   - Keep the real project screens bright and untouched.
   - Use thin yellow/orange rules and small labels to separate projects instead of coloured card backgrounds.
   - Replace dark-purple copy and borders throughout this section.

3. **Services: white**
   - Move the service section from a full purple field to a calm white field with near-black copy.
   - Use purple for headings/actions, yellow for selected callouts, and orange for slim dividers or numbering.
   - Keep cards light and clean so this section resets the eye after Selected Work.

4. **How I think: poppy yellow**
   - Keep this as the yellow chapter, but replace every dark-purple text treatment with near-black.
   - Simplify the step cards to white/near-black details with one purple accent.

5. **Studio and experience: white**
   - Replace beige with white and dark purple with near-black.
   - Preserve Esther’s portrait and the client logos.
   - Use one orange line and small purple labels for personality without filling the section with colour.

6. **Final invitation: poppy orange**
   - Give orange its own confident full-width moment instead of repeating the purple opening.
   - Use near-black text where contrast allows, with white choice panels and a purple primary action.
   - Finish with a near-black footer using white copy and small yellow/orange link states.

```text
PURPLE hero
   ↓
NEAR-BLACK selected work
   ↓
WHITE services
   ↓
YELLOW approach
   ↓
WHITE studio + trust
   ↓
ORANGE final invitation
   ↓
NEAR-BLACK footer
```

## Rest of the site
- Apply the same neutral replacement to case pages, the project-start page, privacy page, and shared overlays: beige becomes white; plum text becomes near-black.
- Let client brand colours remain inside their real logos, screenshots, and case-study brand-detail panels. They are evidence of the work, not part of Es Venture’s interface palette.
- Keep page-specific case imagery and typography unchanged.

## Navigation and interaction
- Update the navigation colour states to match the new section rhythm, including the near-black work section and orange final section.
- Keep yellow as the main action colour on purple/black areas; use purple actions on white/yellow/orange areas.
- Preserve existing motion, layout, typography, content, imagery, language switching, and links.
- Check text contrast, focus states, mobile menus, and section transitions at desktop and phone sizes.

## Technical details
- Replace the beige/paper and plum semantic tokens with true white and near-black roles rather than scattering one-off colour values.
- Promote the existing orange token to the third core brand colour and remove lime/lilac from visible Es Venture interface styling.
- Update shared components and colour-zone tracking so all pages use the same system.
- Verify the homepage and representative inner pages in both languages, then confirm the preview builds without errors.
